Case Study

WEB REDESIGN • UI/UX

IRCTC
Redesigned

The original IRCTC experience contains a lot of useful information, but important actions and features compete for attention across the interface.

The redesign focuses on clearer navigation, a more focused booking flow, and easier access to journey and support actions.

01
BEFORE → AFTER

From information-heavy
to task-focused.

02
WHAT CHANGED

Six changes that
Simplify the experience.

01 Focused Hero

Reworked the hero with clear messaging and focused CTAs for easier understanding.

02 Dedicated Booking

Separated booking from the hero and organized the fields into a clearer flow.

03 Journey Overview

Added a dedicated section to quickly view departure, destination and journey details in one place.

04 Quick Railway Actions

Grouped frequently used railway services for faster access and easier navigation.

05 Structured Support

Added searchable help topics, query submission and tracking in one dedicated section.

06 Clearer FAQ & Footer

Organized FAQs and grouped footer links to make important information easier to find.

03
OUTCOME

Less searching.
More doing.

The redesign makes key railway tasks easier to find, understand
and complete, with less visual clutter along the way.

Clearer navigation
Focused booking
Easier journey actions
Faster support discovery
